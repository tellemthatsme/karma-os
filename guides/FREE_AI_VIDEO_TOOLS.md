## 10. COMPLETE FREE WORKFLOWS

### AI News Video Workflow (Total Cost: $0)
```
1. SCRIPT: Write in Gemini/ChatGPT free tier (2 min)
2. VOICE: Generate narration with edge-tts (unlimited, free) or ElevenLabs (5 min)
3. B-ROLL: Generate 3-5 video clips with Kling/Hailuo/Luma free credits (30-40 min including queue times)
4. SCREEN RECORD: Capture demos with OBS Studio (as needed)
5. EDIT: Cut in DaVinci Resolve, add b-roll + narration (20 min)
6. CAPTIONS: Auto-caption in CapCut (2 min)
7. THUMBNAIL: Generate with ImageFX, edit in Photopea (5 min)
8. UPLOAD: Use DAILY_POSTING_BOARD.md metadata (2 min)

Total: ~70-90 min per video, $0
```

### Music Video Workflow (Total Cost: $0)
```
1. CONCEPT: Script video concept in Claude/Gemini (2 min)
2. VISUALS: Generate key frames with Flux/ComfyUI local (unlimited)
3. VIDEO CLIPS: Animate with Wan 2.1 local or Kling/Hailuo free credits (15 min)
4. LYRICS: Auto-caption lyrics in CapCut (2 min)
5. EDIT: Sync visuals to audio in DaVinci Resolve (30 min)
6. THUMBNAIL: Generate with Flux, edit in Photopea (5 min)
7. UPLOAD: Use DAILY_POSTING_BOARD.md metadata (2 min)

Total: ~55 min per video, $0
```

### YouTube Shorts/Reels Workflow (Total Cost: $0)
```
1. CLIP: Cut 30-55s hook from full track (shorts_cutter.bat already done!)
2. CAPTIONS: Auto-caption in CapCut with style (2 min)
3. MUSIC: Already have your own tracks (no licensing needed)
4. UPLOAD: Vertical 1080x1920 to YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels

Total: ~5 min per Short, $0
```
