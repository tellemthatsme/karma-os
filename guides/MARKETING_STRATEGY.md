### YouTube Community Tab (Start Day 1)
This is one of the MOST powerful growth levers on YouTube. Post 3-5 times per week from day one.

**Post Types:**
| Type | Example | Frequency |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Poll | "Which track hits hardest? MY CHILDREN or DON'T RUSH ME?" | 2x/week |
| Behind-the-scenes | Phone photo of you working + caption about the process | 1x/week |
| Lyrics quote | Screenshot of your best bars + "Who can relate?" | 1x/week |
| New music teaser | 15-second clip + "New track dropping Friday..." | When relevant |
| Fan shoutout | Repost a fan comment + "This is why I do this" | 1x/week |
| Engagement question | "What song got you through your hardest time?" | 1x/week |

**Why this matters:** Community posts get shown to subscribers AND non-subscribers. They drive engagement signals that boost your videos in the algorithm. They keep your channel active between uploads.

### Instagram Reels (Start Day 1)

- Hashtags: 5-10 per Reel (mix of niche + discovery + trending)
- Cross-promote: Share every Reel to your Instagram Story with "New Reel 🔥" sticker

### SEO & Discovery
**YouTube SEO (per video):**
- Title formula: "[CATCHY PHRASE] — [TRACK NAME] (tellemthatsme)"
- Description: First 2 lines = hook (shows in search), then full description
- Tags: 10-12 per video (artist name, genre, track name, mood, Australian hip hop)
- Category: Music

**YouTube End Screens & Cards:**
- End screen (last 20s of every video): Add a Subscribe button + "Next Video" pointing to the next track in the sequence
- **Set up once as a template** in YouTube Studio → Settings → Upload defaults → Advanced → End screen. It auto-applies to all future uploads — no extra time per video.
- Cards: Add 2-3 cards per video linking to related tracks (e.g., MY CHILDREN card on LIKE I MEANT TO DO)
- Cards placement: Show at the moment you mention the related theme (e.g., card for WEATHER YOU CAN DO appears when you talk about storms)

**YouTube Shorts SEO:**
- Title: Start with a hook ("This one's for my kids..." or "Nobody caught these bars...")
- #Shorts hashtag in title OR description (YouTube recommends one, not both)
- Use trending audio tags when applicable
- Pin a comment with a call-to-action: "Full song on my channel — link in bio"
- Post Shorts at peak times: 12PM and 7PM AEST

**Hashtag Strategy:**
- Always use: #tellemthatsme #AustralianHipHop #NewMusic2026
- Track-specific: #[TrackName] #OriginalMusic
- Discovery: #IndependentArtist #UndergroundHipHop #RealTalk #NewMusic
- Trending: Check weekly trending hashtags and include 1-2

## EMERGENCY PLAYBOOKS

### "My video got zero views"
1. Check if it's indexed (search the exact title on YouTube)
2. Share to all social platforms immediately
3. Post in 5 more Facebook groups
4. Create a Short from the best 30 seconds
5. Engage with 20 similar videos in your niche

### "I'm losing subscribers"
1. Check YouTube Analytics for the unsubscribe spike
2. Look at which video caused it
3. Adjust content style (maybe too much self-promotion?)
4. Post more value content (tutorials, behind-the-scenes)
5. Ask your audience what they want

### "YouTube rejected my monetization"
1. Check for copyright claims on any videos
2. Ensure all content is original (no copied beats without license)
3. Wait 30 days and reapply
4. Continue posting — more content = stronger application

### "Bridge/Upload system breaks"
1. Restart bridge: double-click `start.bat`
2. Check extension is installed: `chrome://extensions`
3. Check bridge status: `http://127.0.0.1:9876/status`
4. Fall back to manual upload using DAILY_POSTING_BOARD.html
5. Check MCP config: `%APPDATA%\Claude\claude_desktop_config.json`
