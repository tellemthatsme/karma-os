# AI News Video Production Skill

## What This Does

Manages the end-to-end workflow for the KARMA OS AI news YouTube channel — from research brief to published video to social media distribution.

## Content Strategy

Based on `ai_news/AI_NEWS_CHANNEL.md`:

| Day | Content Type | Source |
|-----|-------------|--------|
| Monday | Breaking News | `CURRENT_AI_BRIEF.md` §1 (Top 5 Stories) |
| Tuesday | AI Coding Update | `CURRENT_AI_BRIEF.md` §3 (Dev Tools) |
| Wednesday | Tool Review | Pick 1-2 tools from AWESOME_REPOS.md |
| Thursday | Agent Framework Deep-Dive | `CURRENT_AI_BRIEF.md` §4 (Agent Frameworks) |
| Friday | GitHub Trending | `CURRENT_AI_BRIEF.md` §5 (Trending Repos) |
| Saturday | Short clip / Thread | Extract 1 story for TikTok/X thread |
| Sunday | Weekly Roundup | `CURRENT_AI_BRIEF.md` full brief |

## Video Format Templates

### Breaking News (3-5 min)
```
HOOK (0-15s): "[Company] just [did what] — here's what you need to know"
CONTEXT (15-60s): What was announced, key specs
WHY IT MATTERS (1-3min): Real implications, comparisons
WHAT TO DO (3-4min): Try this, avoid that, watch for this
CTA (4-5min): Subscribe, comment your take
```

### Weekly Roundup (8-12 min)
```
INTRO (0-30s): "Here's everything that happened in AI this week"
TOP 5 (30s-8min): Numbered list with brief coverage
DEEP DIVE (8-10min): One story that deserves more attention
PREVIEW (10-11min): What to watch next week
CTA (11-12min): Subscribe, bell icon
```

## Thumbnail Formula

- Background: Dark gradient (navy, black, deep purple)
- Main Element: Company logo or tool screenshot
- Text: 3-5 words MAX, large sans-serif, white/yellow on dark
- NO rage faces, clickbait arrows, excessive emojis
