## 3. Valuation Methods

### Method 1: Revenue Multiple (Conservative)
**Assumption:** $500/mo combined revenue (Year 1)
- 12x annual multiple: **$72,000 valuation**
- 24x annual multiple (growth): **$144,000 valuation**

### Method 2: Asset-Based
**Valuation of owned assets:**
- 16 original tracks (catalog value): $5K–$50K (at $300–$3,000 per track)
- Content ID infrastructure: $2K–$5K
- Browser extension system: $5K–$15K
- **Total: $12K–$70K**

### Method 3: IP + Platform Valuation
**Intellectual property + user base:**
- Music catalog (16 tracks, perpetual income): $10K–$50K
- YouTube channels (potential brand value): $5K–$20K
- Software tools (bridge, dashboards, automation): $10K–$30K
- Facebook groups (future community asset): $2K–$10K
- **Total: $27K–$110K**
