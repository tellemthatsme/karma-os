# AI NEWS CHANNEL — Content Strategy & Toolkit

## Channel: "AI DAILY" (or your chosen name)
**Format:** Straight-to-the-point AI & AI coding news
**Style:** No fluff. What happened. Why it matters. What to do about it.

---

## Content Pillars

### 1. Breaking AI News (2-3x/week)
- Major model releases (GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama)
- Company announcements (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Meta)
- Industry shifts (funding, acquisitions, policy)

### 2. AI Coding Updates (2x/week)
- New coding tools & IDEs (Cursor, Copilot, Windsurf)
- Open-source releases (frameworks, models, libraries)
- Coding benchmarks & comparisons

### 3. AI Tools & Workflows (1-2x/week)
- Tool tutorials (5-min walkthroughs)
- Workflow automations
- "I tried X so you don't have to"

### 4. Weekly Roundup (1x/week — Sunday)
- Top 5 things that happened in AI this week
- What to watch next week

---

## Video Format Templates

### Breaking News (3-5 min)
```
HOOK (0-15s): "OpenAI just dropped GPT-5 and here's what you need to know"
CONTEXT (15-60s): What was announced, key specs
WHY IT MATTERS (1-3min): Real implications, comparisons
WHAT TO DO (3-4min): Try this, avoid that, watch for this
CTA (4-5min): Subscribe, comment your take
```

### Tool Review (5-8 min)
```
HOOK (0-15s): "This free AI tool just replaced my $50/month subscription"
DEMO (15s-3min): Screen recording of the tool in action
VERDICT (3-5min): Pros, cons, who it's for
ALTERNATIVES (5-6min): Quick comparison
CTA (6-8min): Link in description, subscribe
```

### Weekly Roundup (8-12 min)
```
INTRO (0-30s): "Here's everything that happened in AI this week"
TOP 5 (30s-8min): Numbered list with brief coverage
DEEP DIVE (8-10min): One story that deserves more attention
PREVIEW (10-11min): What to watch next week
CTA (11-12min): Subscribe, bell icon
```

## Thumbnail Formula

- **Background:** Dark gradient or solid color (navy, black, deep purple)
- **Main Element:** Company logo or tool screenshot
- **Text:** 3-5 words MAX, large sans-serif font
- **Colors:** High contrast — white/yellow text on dark background
- **NO:** Rage faces, clickbait arrows, excessive emojis

### Thumbnail Text Examples
- "GPT-5 IS HERE"
- "FREE Claude Alternative"
- "This Changes Everything"
- "Don't Use This Yet"
- "I Tested It For You"
- "5 AI Tools This Week"

### TikTok/Reels Format (30-60s)
```
HOOK (0-3s): Text overlay + voiceover
DEMO (3-20s): Screen recording of the tool/news
TAKEAWAY (20-25s): "Here's why this matters"
CTA (25-30s): "Follow for daily AI updates"
```

### Instagram Carousel Format
- Slide 1: Hook headline
- Slide 2-4: Key points with visuals
- Slide 5: Takeaway / opinion
- Slide 6: CTA (follow, link in bio)
